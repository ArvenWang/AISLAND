Outdoor Tileset by Calciumtrice
--------------------------------------------

An outdoor tileset for beach and woods environments.

Included are:

    Beach environment spritesheet - includes ground, water, rocks, grass, containers and driftwood and cliffs
    Forest environment spritesheet - includes ground, grass, mud, rocks, containers and gear, animated fire and cliffs
    Trees spritesheet - trunk and canopy combo variants (including no canopy)
    Water spritesheet - animated version of the beach's water tiles - maybe looped through on all tiles to create the appearance of waves (available as a single spritesheet and individual sheets for each frame)

Tiles with transparent backgrounds are intended to be layered above other tiles.

Made to pair with the 4 Direction Animated Warrior, also available on OpenGameArt.org.
